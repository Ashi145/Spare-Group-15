About us

Kampala Auto Parts & Accessories Centre supplies vehicle spare parts and accessories in Kampala and surrounding areas. Until now, customers had to visit, call or send WhatsApp messages to learn if a part was available. This website puts that information online.

Products and services

Spare parts for brakes, engines, suspension, electrical systems and filters; oils and fluids; car accessories; and delivery.

Customers we serve

Individual vehicle owners, garages, mechanics and transport companies.

Benefits to customers

Check price and stock before travelling, find parts that suit your vehicle, order from your phone and follow your order status.

Our vision

To be the most trusted source of vehicle parts in Kampala, known for honest information and quick service.

Our mission

To make buying spare parts simple by giving every customer clear prices, accurate availability and reliable delivery.
